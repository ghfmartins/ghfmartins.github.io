From law school to code

I am a software engineer focused on native mobile development. I started as an Android intern in 2021 and have been building and maintaining production apps ever since, mostly in Kotlin and Java, and also on iOS with Swift, SwiftUI and UIKit.

I care about well-designed architecture and code the next developer can read. At LG lugar de gente I led the migration of the Gen.te Mobile and Feedback Gen.te Android apps to the architecture Google recommends. At Taqtile I started on iOS and became the main owner of the app’s Android side, with Claude Code part of the team’s daily workflow.

Before tech, I earned a law degree from UFG and worked as a lawyer. That background left me careful with requirements, clear in writing and comfortable talking to business teams.
